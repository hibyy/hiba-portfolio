Sign Language Production & Recognition

A bidirectional text/voice ↔ sign language system (final-year project). I led the pose-to-video phase: mapped a custom 151-joint skeleton to MediaPipe Holistic, tested Pix2Pix GAN and ControlNet + AnimateDiff, then settled on ControlNet + Stable Diffusion 1.5, optimized to run on a single T4 GPU.

Warehouse Robotics Simulation

A simulated robot arm in PyBullet. YOLOv8, trained on synthetic data, finds the objects from an overhead camera, and an LLM with rule-based planning turns instructions like "stack the green cube on the red cube" into pick-and-place actions. Ongoing.

StethoSpeech

A silent speech interface that turns vibrations behind the ear, recorded with an ordinary clinical stethoscope, into natural-sounding speech. It aims to help people with voice disorders and to allow discreet communication, and it still works with loud music in the background.

Research by Neil Shah et al., IIIT Hyderabad. Published in ACM IMWUT, 2024.
